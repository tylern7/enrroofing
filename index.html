About

A company built on roofing, and on family

Both heads of the Virga and Turik families worked in the Canadian commercial, industrial and institutional roofing industry for one of Canada's leading roofing contractors after immigrating to Canada in 1956.

Paul Virga met the standards for Gold Seal Certification in the Canadian roofing industry and was a foreman for nearly 45 years. Illes Turik worked in the industry for 40 years.

Together with their sons, they ran, oversaw and worked on projects for Chrysler, Ford and General Motors, including plant maintenance shutdowns, plant additions and new plant roof construction.

Today, ENR Roofing Inc. is led by Eddie Turik and Robert and Peter Virga, bringing that same experience and knowledge to your building.
